## Featured projects

### [JOAN](https://github.com/Yoanybx/joan) — agent-to-agent payments protocol *(open source, Rust)*
A language and protocol for verifiable machine-to-machine payments between AI agents: an agent can pay another agent for a service and both sides get a cryptographically verifiable receipt of delivery. 26 crates, full test suite, Apache-2.0.

### [VerzaTrade](https://verzatrade.com) — real-time quantitative trading platform *(production)*
Multitenant platform that runs Pine Script v6 strategies on live streaming market data, with broker API integrations, execution and risk controls, billing, and an in-platform AI assistant. A fail-closed data guardian validates every tick and candle and quarantines bad data before it can drive any decision. Entire stack on Cloudflare, deployed and verified live after every release.

### [Minogan](https://minogan.ai) — AI-agent risk scoring for insurers *(production)*
Insurtech SaaS that scores AI-agent risk for underwriters and produces explainable Bind / Bind-with-terms / Refer recommendations a human reviews — "recommend, don't insure" by design. Agents analyze portfolios and exposures and produce evidence packs with full audit trails.

### [SkillCrew](https://skillcrewusa.com) — recycling & import platform with AI agents *(production)*
Customer-facing platform whose production AI assistant serves real buyers in Spanish, English and Portuguese: product identification from customer photos, lead capture and qualification, and pricing published daily across 90+ brand catalogs — all integrated with the live operation, not a demo.

### [alphanex-ti](https://github.com/Yoanybx/alphanex-ti) — threat intelligence *(open source)*
Documented analysis of the fake-developer-job campaign ("Contagious Interview") attributed to Lazarus/TraderTraitor, including indicators and the exact malware sample preserved for security teams and authorities.
